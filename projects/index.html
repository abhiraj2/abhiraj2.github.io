research and engineering projects, in descending order of complexity.

WR-Raft

Paper in preparation

A Raft variant that weights quorum participation by each follower’s observed fsync latency, tracked as an exponentially weighted moving average. The work includes an analysis against Cabinet (VLDB ’25) and WOC and documents the correctness hazards of weighted quorums. I mentor three interns on the project.

Raft Consensus Replication Storage

Automated Live Migration

Published · IEEE ICBDA 2025

Live migration of Docker containers across cloud providers using iterative checkpointing. My contribution: the provider-selection criteria and the stop algorithm for iterative checkpointing.

GPU Options Pricer

A Monte Carlo engine built on Apple Metal compute kernels. It prices 10,000 options about 300× faster than the serial CPU baseline and is validated against the closed-form solution to under 0.05% error.
