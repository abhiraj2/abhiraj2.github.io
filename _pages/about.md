I am a systems engineer who does research. I work as an Engineering Analyst at Goldman Sachs on distributed systems for Listed Derivatives, and I do research at PES University on consensus protocols and database security.

My research started at IISc, where I worked on UNMASQUE under Prof. Jayant Haritsa, and continued with a paper on live migration of Docker containers across cloud providers, published at IEEE ICBDA 2025. Today I work with CCBD at PES on WR-Raft, a Raft variant that weights quorum participation by observed follower fsync latency, and mentor three interns on it. I am also studying timing side channels in row-level security.

## Research interests

- **Distributed systems:** consensus and replication, and how they behave on real, uneven hardware.
- **Storage:** how storage latency shapes the performance and correctness of replicated systems.
- **Database security:** side channels in access-control mechanisms such as row-level security.
